## Campaign Management
- Thinkbyte.ai plans to launch two campaigns: one targeting Shopify users with quarterly newsletters, and another for influencer outreach using email retargeting on platforms like Instagram and TikTok.
- Mailmodo will be used for Shopify integration due to its native support, while alternative tools will be considered for influencer outreach to address spam risks. Charan expressed need for clear communication to avoid future misunderstandings.
- Concerns arose regarding potential spam issues and domain reputation risks with influencer outreach campaign. Miscommunication about email marketing process and campaign requirements led to confusion.

## SMTP Management
- Challenges highlighted in managing AWS accounts for SMTP configuration, along with implications of shared IPs on domain reputation. Charan noted Netcore provided more comprehensive support for managing these technical aspects.
- Guidance will be provided on setting up AWS for SMTP use while maintaining domain reputation. Mailmodo does not offer direct management of AWS accounts, unlike Netcore, which offers more extensive support.
- Discussion focused on managing SMTP servers and IPs for email campaigns, particularly regarding shared versus dedicated IPs and server management.

## Pain Point
- The issue is that Mailmodo SMTP is typically used for transactional and promotional emails, not for cold outreach emails.
- They do not engage in setting up or managing the AWS account or Cloudflare.
- Many loan clients primarily use only a couple of tools for cold outreach, despite the availability of more options.
- Clients demanded the ability to send cold outreach emails and use a mail model for easier template creation and campaign management, leading to the integration of SMTP options.
