## Demand Planning Process Automation
- Harish Byrappa from EatFit discussed their current demand planning process, involving multiple modules: t+6, t+4, t+1, each for different time frames. The process relies heavily on Excel and includes manual tasks like updating SKU statuses, which affect demand forecasting for kitchens.
- Automation of SKU status updates is considered to eliminate manual errors, save time. This includes automating identification of live SKUs at each kitchen.
- Manual demand planning leads to inaccuracies, as subjective judgment is used for alternate demand planning. This is necessary to ensure SKUs that haven't sold recently still get planned demand based on their class and historical performance.
- Explore automating demand planning to improve efficiency and accuracy. This could involve using more advanced logic or tools to take over repetitive tasks currently handled manually.
- The current process is manual, causing inefficiencies. Updating SKU statuses by hand leads to potential errors and takes substantial time, especially when handling large Excel files exceeding 100 MB. This makes day-to-day operations cumbersome and prone to inaccuracies.

## Excel File Management
- Manual handling prone to errors, affecting data accuracy and decision-making, particularly around updating kitchen-level SKU statuses and demand forecasts.
- Consider automation to reduce file size, improve process efficiency by replacing manual updates with automated systems. This could involve implementing formulas that dynamically update based on real-time data inputs.
- The demand planning process generates large Excel files due to manual data handling, including managing SKU statuses across multiple kitchens and calculating demand forecasts. This results in files that are difficult to manage and prone to errors.
- Large Excel files cause data management challenges, making it difficult to efficiently update, calculate, and manage demand planning data. This leads to a time-consuming process and potential data integrity issues.
