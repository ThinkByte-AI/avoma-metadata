## Target Audience
- Education is key; users need to understand the app's meditation practices.
- Features that help with specific tasks (like cooking) are highly valued.
- Targeting both experienced users and newcomers to mindfulness.
- Stressed individuals or high-stress jobs may be a focus group.
- Personalized activity generation and guidance are crucial for user engagement.

## Key Metrics
- DAU is essential; users interact daily.
- Metrics to monitor include retention, duration, and frequency of app usage.
- Need to track session counts as a key success metric for the MVP.
- Set a baseline retention rate that signifies MVP success.

## Pricing
- Consider introducing a freemium tier before full paid options, as there will be no in-app purchases or ads.
- Benefit: Freemium models attract more users by offering a trial without commitment.
- Pricing should reflect the value users place on key features and their willingness to pay, which can vary across different user cohorts.

## Monetization Strategy
- Focus on a freemium model for initial attraction, transitioning to paid later.
