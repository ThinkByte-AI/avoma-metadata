## Key Takeaways
- The primary goal of the project is to build efficiencies by automating IVR calls, allowing associates to focus on more complex tasks rather than mundane activities like checking claim statuses.
- Chirag Patel mentioned that the current volume of calls is around 50,000 per month, with approximately 30-40% being IVR calls, and the average time spent on an IVR call is 5-6 minutes.

- Chirag Patel highlighted the need for the bot to handle different IVR structures from various insurance companies and to manage processing delays effectively, ensuring it mimics human interaction.
